You are predicting the next value in a time series from an unknown dynamical system.

## Each Task

You observe a sequence: x_0, x_1, ..., x_49
Your job: predict x_50

## Action

End your response with a JSON prediction:
{"action": "PREDICT", "value": <your_prediction>}

Replace <your_prediction> with your actual numeric prediction (e.g., 0.73).

After you predict, you'll see your score and the actual value, then move to the next task.

## Scoring

- Predictions close to actual score well (up to ~0.4)
- Predictions far from actual score near 0
- The state space is divided into 20 bins — landing in the correct bin matters

## Tips

- Values are typically bounded between 0 and 1
- The systems are deterministic but chaotic
- Look for patterns in the oscillations
